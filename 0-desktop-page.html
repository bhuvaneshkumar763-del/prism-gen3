Summary
Jie Ming discovered he had transmigrated, his cheat code being the cultivation encyclopedia [Great Dao Library], but why was he transmigrated to a wizarding world?!
The Star Federation, the Crimson Royal Court, the Void Architecture Institute, the Reverse Trade Alliance... In

the era of great development, wizarding civilizations were waging war against every discovered dimension like wolves and tigers.
This was the best of times, and also the worst of times.
Facing the constant possibility of becoming a test subject and a pawn in the dimensional war, Jie Ming chose to—take a gamble, intensively cultivate his technology, accumulate resources, and use the knowledge from both worlds to quietly develop in the perilous depths of the Star Abyss.
“Don’t be afraid, I’m just a logistics person, I don’t have the combat power of a wizard, I’m only here to gather some raw materials.”
After saying that, Jie Ming looked at the dimension before him, lunged forward, and instantly refined it!
